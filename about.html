ABOUT ALFISHA E-STORE

Technology.
Made Simple.

ALFISHA E-STORE is a modern electronics e-commerce website designed to make discovering, comparing and purchasing technology products simple and convenient.

WHO WE ARE

Building a Better Shopping Experience.

ALFISHA E-STORE is a student-developed electronics e-commerce website created with the goal of providing users with a clean, organized and professional online shopping experience.

Our website brings different categories of electronic products together in one place. Users can browse products, view detailed information, add products to their cart, save products to a wishlist and manage their shopping experience easily.

The project combines front-end design, interactive functionality and responsive web development to create a complete e-commerce experience.

OUR PROJECT

What We Developed

ALFISHA E-STORE was developed as a complete front-end e-commerce project with multiple pages and interactive features.

Home Page

A professional landing page introducing ALFISHA E-STORE, highlighting featured products, categories and the shopping experience.

Product Catalog

A structured product catalog where users can browse electronic products by category and explore available items.

Product Details

Detailed product pages provide users with product images, descriptions, prices, ratings and purchasing options.

Shopping Cart

Users can add products to their cart, change quantities, remove products and view their order summary.

Wishlist

Products can be saved to a wishlist so users can easily return to products they are interested in.

User Profile

A dedicated profile area provides users with an organized interface for their account and shopping-related information.

Services Page

Information about delivery, secure payments, returns, support and other customer services is presented clearly.

About Page

The About section introduces the project, development team, technologies and purpose of ALFISHA E-STORE.

Responsive Design

The website is designed to adapt to desktops, tablets and mobile screens for a consistent user experience.

TECHNOLOGY STACK
